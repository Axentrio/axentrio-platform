# Connecting your booking calendar

Bookings run on Axentrio's built-in scheduler. Connect one calendar and the bot offers your real free slots during chats, writes each confirmed appointment to that calendar, and never double-books over events already on it.

**Tier:** requires Pro or Enterprise. On Essential, the *Bookings* sidebar item shows a Pro lock.

**Choose one calendar — Google or Outlook:**
1. Open *Bookings* in the sidebar and start *Setup* (or *Settings → Scheduler*).
2. Click **Connect Google Calendar** or **Connect Outlook Calendar**. You are redirected to Google or Microsoft to authorize Axentrio.
3. After you approve, the calendar shows as *Connected* with the account email. The bot now reads availability from it and books into it.

A bot uses one calendar at a time — Google or Outlook, not both.

**If it says "Reconnect needed":** the link expired or access was revoked, so the bot cannot read availability and falls back to capturing requests. Click **Reconnect** to restore booking — it is a re-authorize, not a fresh setup.

**Disconnecting:** open the scheduler settings and disconnect. Existing appointments stay on your calendar; the bot just stops offering new slots.

> Axentrio does not use Cal.com. Bookings sync to your own Google or Outlook calendar through the built-in scheduler.
